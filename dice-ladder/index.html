Privacy Policy for Dice Roller & Decision Maker

Last updated: September 2026

Dice Roller & Decision Maker is designed to be private by default. There are no analytics and no user accounts. Free users see a small, non-personalized ad banner; the optional one-time Pro purchase removes ads.

What we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, and the app contains no analytics SDK.

For free users, the app shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure that ad, Google may access a device advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We request non-personalized ads only, and no account or sign-in is required. After you buy the one-time Pro upgrade, no ad requests are made.

What stays on your device

Your app settings and, if enabled, your result history are stored only in local device storage. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can clear history at any time from the History tab.

Network

All features work offline. Normal use makes no network requests; the only network activity is the ad banner shown to free users and purchase validation.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; we never see them. The app stores only a local flag indicating whether Pro is unlocked. Restoring a purchase contacts the store and the purchase-management provider (RevenueCat) using an anonymous device identifier — no name, email, or account is required.

Optional feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the message you type (and an optional name) is submitted to our feedback form so we can read it. This is entirely optional and never happens automatically.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
