Privacy Policy

USA States Quiz (Android) — last updated September 2026

This app is developed and published by dombuildsdev ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

  • In-app purchases: provided through RevenueCat and Google Play Billing. Purchase and entitlement status is processed by these providers; we do not see or store your payment details.
  • Advertising: the free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect a mobile advertising ID to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies and Google ad settings.
  • Quiz progress & preferences: stored locally on your device only (e.g. theme, settings, learning progress).
  • State dataset: the 50-state flag/capital dataset is bundled with the app; the app performs no remote dataset checks and sends no personally identifiable information.

What we do not do

We do not require an account, do not collect your name/email/location, and do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can reset in-app data or uninstall the app at any time. You can opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings or Google ad settings.
