Privacy Policy for Eye Break

Last updated: September 2026

Eye Break is a screen-break reminder based on the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. It is built to be private by default. There are no analytics and no user accounts. Free users see a small banner ad; the optional one-time Pro purchase removes ads.

What we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data, and the app contains no analytics SDK.

For free users, the app shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure that ad, Google may access a device advertising identifier and related device data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We request non-personalized ads only, and no account or sign-in is required. After you buy the one-time Pro upgrade, no ad requests are made.

What stays on your device

Your settings, break log, streaks, daily goal and reminder preferences are stored only in local device storage. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Notifications & alarms

Break reminders are scheduled locally on your device using the operating system's alarm and notification services. Nothing is sent to any server. The Done / Start break / Snooze actions on a notification are handled entirely on your device, even when the app is closed.

Backup

You may export your settings and break history to a file on your device (or a service you choose) and import them back later. The export file is written only where you choose to save it; we never see it.

Network

The timer and reminders work entirely offline. Normal use makes no network requests; the only network activity is the ad banner shown to free users, purchase validation, and the optional feedback form you explicitly submit.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). Payment details are handled entirely by the store; we never see them. The app stores only a local flag indicating whether Pro is unlocked. Restoring a purchase contacts the store and the purchase-management provider (RevenueCat) using an anonymous device identifier - no name, email, or account is required.

Optional feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the message you type (and an optional name) is submitted to our feedback form so we can read it. This is entirely optional and never happens automatically.

Not medical advice

Eye Break is a reminder tool, not medical advice or a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Persistent eye pain, headaches, or vision changes should be checked by an optometrist or doctor.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
